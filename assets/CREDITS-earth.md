# Earth texture credits

Earth night and cloud textures by [Solar System Scope](https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/), licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

- Source assets: `2k_earth_nightmap.jpg`, `2k_earth_clouds.jpg`
- The 2048 × 1024 source maps were downscaled to 1024 × 512 for a background-only WebGL globe; local filenames were shortened.
- Textures are visual reconstruction maps based on NASA imagery; they are not unmodified spacecraft photographs.
